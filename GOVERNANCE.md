# Governance

ssh-mcp is maintained by a single maintainer today, with the aim of growing a
maintainer group as the project matures.

| Role | Who | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Lead maintainer | @nguyencongnamit | merge, release, decide licensing and roadmap, final say on security changes |
| Maintainer | (open) | review and merge, triage, cut patch releases |
| Contributor | anyone who signed the CLA | open issues and pull requests |

**Decisions.** Day-to-day decisions happen in pull requests. Changes to the
security boundary (helper, certificate format, sandbox, transport, auth) need
an issue with a written design first and a maintainer approval.

**Becoming a maintainer.** Sustained, high-quality contributions and reviews
over several months, nominated by the lead maintainer.

**Licensing.** The Licensor (Nguyen Cong Nam) holds the right to offer
commercial licenses. Every version converts to Apache-2.0 three years after its
release; that conversion cannot be revoked.
